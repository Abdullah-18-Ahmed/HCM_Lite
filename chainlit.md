# HCM Lite

> **Demo data — fictional.** All people, figures and policies in this application are
> generated demo data. They do not describe any real organization or person.

HCM Lite is a basic human capital management app with an AI HR assistant on the same screen.
Sign in with your HCM Lite account. What you can see depends on your role
(Employee, Manager or HR Admin).

## What you can do

- **Role dashboard**: your own profile, leave balances and requests; team attendance and pending
  leave for managers; organization-wide headcount, attendance, leave and payroll totals for HR
  admins.
- **HR policy questions**: answers grounded in the HR document library, with sources. If a
  question isn't covered, the assistant says it could not be verified.
- **Exact HR figures**: headcount, attendance, late arrivals, leave and payroll summaries, taken
  from the HR database and limited to what your role may see.
- **Your leave balance**: your own remaining leave by type.
- **Leave requests**: the assistant prepares a draft and checks it. Nothing is submitted until
  you press **Confirm**. Submitted requests are stored as *pending*.
- **Dashboard summary**: a short AI summary that uses only your dashboard figures.
